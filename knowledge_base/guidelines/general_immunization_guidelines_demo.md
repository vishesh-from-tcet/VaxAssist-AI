# General Immunization Safety, Handling, and Clinical Guidelines (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: This document contains curated DEMO immunization reference guidelines for development and testing of the VaxAssist AI platform. It is not intended for direct clinical prescription.

## 1. Vaccine Storage and Cold Chain Integrity
Vaccines are biological products that lose potency when exposed to temperatures outside the recommended range (+2°C to +8°C for most refrigerated vaccines, or -20°C / -70°C for ultracold mRNA formulations).
- **Cold Chain Maintenance**: Refrigerator temperatures must be monitored twice daily.
- **Freeze-Sensitive Vaccines**: Vaccines containing aluminum adjuvants (such as DTP, Hepatitis B, and Tdap) must never be frozen. The "Shake Test" is used to detect freeze damage.
- **Light Sensitivity**: Vaccines such as BCG, MMR, and Varicella must be protected from direct sunlight and ultraviolet exposure.

## 2. Pre-Vaccination Assessment & Screenings
Healthcare professionals must conduct a routine pre-vaccination assessment before administering any immunobiological agent:
- **Severe Acute Illness**: Moderate or severe acute illness with or without fever is a precaution for vaccination. Mild illness (e.g., mild upper respiratory infection or diarrhea) is NOT a contraindication.
- **Anaphylaxis History**: A history of severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to a previous dose or component of a vaccine is an absolute contraindication to that specific vaccine.
- **Immunocompromised Patients**: Live attenuated vaccines (such as MMR, Varicella, Yellow Fever, and BCG) should generally be avoided in severely immunocompromised individuals unless specifically indicated by a specialist.

## 3. Administration Technique and Post-Vaccination Observation
- **Intramuscular (IM)**: Administer into the anterolateral aspect of the thigh for infants, or the deltoid muscle for older children and adults at a 90-degree angle.
- **Subcutaneous (SC)**: Administer into the outer upper arm or anterolateral thigh at a 45-degree angle (e.g., MMR, Varicella).
- **Post-Vaccination Observation**: Patients must be observed for at least 15 minutes after vaccination to monitor for immediate hypersensitivity reactions or vasovagal syncope.
