# Poliomyelitis Vaccines: OPV and IPV (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Vaccine Types
- **Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV - Bivalent bOPV)**: Live attenuated Sabin strains (Types 1 and 3). Induces strong mucosal gut immunity, preventing wild poliovirus transmission in communities.
- **Inactivated Polio Vaccine (IPV / fIPV)**: Salk killed poliovirus strains (Types 1, 2, 3) administered intramuscularly or fractional intradermally. Eliminates the risk of vaccine-associated paralytic poliomyelitis (VAPP).

## Sequential Schedule in Universal Immunization
- Birth: OPV Zero dose.
- 6 weeks: OPV 1 + Fractional IPV (fIPV 1).
- 10 weeks: OPV 2.
- 14 weeks: OPV 3 + Fractional IPV (fIPV 2).
- 9 months: fIPV 3 booster dose.
