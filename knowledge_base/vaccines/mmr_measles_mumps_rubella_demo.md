# MMR (Measles, Mumps, and Rubella) Vaccine (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Clinical Indication
MMR is a lyophilized preparation of live attenuated measles, mumps, and rubella viruses. It provides long-lasting protective immunity against measles complications (such as subacute sclerosing panencephalitis and severe pneumonia), mumps orchitis/meningitis, and congenital rubella syndrome.

## Dosing Schedule
- **Dose 1**: Administered at 9 to 12 months (or 12-15 months depending on national epidemiological recommendations).
- **Dose 2**: Administered at 16 to 24 months (or 4-6 years of age prior to school entry).
- **Minimum Interval**: At least 4 weeks between doses.

## Egg Allergy Safety
Extensive clinical studies demonstrate that MMR can be safely administered to children with non-anaphylactic or even severe egg allergies without prior skin testing, because the vaccine viruses are grown in chick embryo fibroblast cultures and contain negligible ovalbumin.
