# Seasonal Influenza Quadrivalent Vaccination (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Composition and Strain Updates
Influenza vaccines are reformulated annually to match circulating strains of Influenza A (H1N1, H3N2) and Influenza B (Yamagata, Victoria lineages).

## Timing and Frequency
- Recommended annually before the start of the local influenza season (Autumn/Winter or pre-monsoon depending on geographic hemisphere).
- **Pediatric First Dose Rule**: Children aged 6 months to 8 years receiving the influenza vaccine for the very first time require 2 doses spaced at least 4 weeks apart for optimal priming.
