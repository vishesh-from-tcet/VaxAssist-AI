# Hepatitis B Vaccine Clinical Summary (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Overview and Efficacy
Hepatitis B vaccine is a recombinant DNA vaccine containing purified Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg). It produces protective antibody titers (anti-HBs >= 10 mIU/mL) in over 95% of healthy infants, children, and young adults.

## Schedule Recommendations
- **Birth Dose**: Administered monovalent within 24 hours of birth to prevent perinatal transmission from HBsAg-positive mothers.
- **Primary Series**: Completed as a 3-dose or 4-dose schedule (e.g. Birth, 6 weeks, 10 weeks, 14 weeks as part of Pentavalent vaccine, or 0, 1, 6 months).
- **Minimum Intervals**:
  - Dose 1 to Dose 2: 4 weeks.
  - Dose 2 to Dose 3: 8 weeks (with Dose 3 administered at least 16 weeks after Dose 1 and at least 24 weeks of age).

## Precautions & Side Effects
Most common reactions are mild local tenderness at the injection site and low-grade fever lasting 24-48 hours.
