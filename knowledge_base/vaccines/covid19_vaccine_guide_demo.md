# COVID-19 Vaccination & Booster Guidance (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Vaccine Platforms
- **mRNA Vaccines**: Encapsulated nucleoside-modified mRNA encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein (e.g., Pfizer-BioNTech Comirnaty, Moderna Spikevax).
- **Viral Vector Vaccines**: Recombinant non-replicating adenoviral vectors (e.g., Oxford-AstraZeneca / Covishield).
- **Inactivated / Protein Subunit**: Whole virion inactivated (Covaxin) or recombinant spike protein with adjuvant (Novavax).

## Administration Schedules & Boosters
- **Primary Series**: 2 doses spaced 3 to 8 weeks apart depending on vaccine platform.
- **Updated Boosters**: Recommended 6 to 12 months after primary series or last booster, especially for high-risk populations, elderly, and healthcare workers.

## Co-administration with Other Vaccines
COVID-19 vaccines may be administered concurrently with other age-appropriate vaccines (including seasonal influenza) at different anatomical injection sites.
