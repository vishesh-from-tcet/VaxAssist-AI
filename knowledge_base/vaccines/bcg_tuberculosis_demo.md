# BCG (Bacille Calmette-Guérin) Vaccine Overview (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Indication and Purpose
BCG is a live attenuated bacterial vaccine derived from *Mycobacterium bovis*. It provides high protection against severe and disseminated forms of childhood tuberculosis, particularly tuberculous meningitis and miliary tuberculosis in infants.

## Schedule and Dosing
- **Target Age**: Administered as early as possible after birth (within 24 hours to 1 year of age).
- **Dose**:
  - Neonates under 1 month: 0.05 mL.
  - Infants 1 month to 1 year: 0.1 mL.
- **Route & Site**: Strict intradermal injection into the left upper arm over the insertion of the deltoid muscle.

## Expected Reaction & Healing Process
A papule develops at the injection site 2 to 3 weeks after vaccination, which may ulcerate and heal slowly over 6 to 12 weeks leaving a small permanent scar. No topical medications or bandages should be applied.

## Contraindications
- Symptomatic HIV infection or known severe congenital immunodeficiency.
- Generalized septicemia or severe skin lesions at the injection site.
