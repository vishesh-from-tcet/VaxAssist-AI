# National Immunization Schedule (UIP) Reference (DEMO)

> [!NOTE]
> DEMO NOTICE: Strictly labeled as demo clinical data for testing VaxAssist AI RAG search and retrieval.

## Standard Milestone Table
1. **At Birth**:
   - BCG (0.05 mL, Intradermal, Left upper arm)
   - Hepatitis B Birth dose (0.5 mL, IM, Anterolateral mid-thigh)
   - OPV-0 (2 drops, Oral)
2. **At 6 Weeks**:
   - Pentavalent-1 (DTP + HepB + Hib - 0.5 mL, IM)
   - OPV-1 (2 drops, Oral)
   - Rotavirus-1 (5 drops, Oral)
   - Fractional IPV-1 (0.1 mL, Intradermal)
   - PCV-1 (0.5 mL, IM)
3. **At 10 Weeks**:
   - Pentavalent-2 (0.5 mL, IM)
   - OPV-2 (2 drops, Oral)
   - Rotavirus-2 (5 drops, Oral)
4. **At 14 Weeks**:
   - Pentavalent-3 (0.5 mL, IM)
   - OPV-3 (2 drops, Oral)
   - Rotavirus-3 (5 drops, Oral)
   - Fractional IPV-2 (0.1 mL, Intradermal)
   - PCV-2 (0.5 mL, IM)
5. **At 9 to 12 Months**:
   - Measles-Rubella (MR) 1st dose (0.5 mL, SC)
   - JE-1 (in endemic districts)
   - PCV Booster (0.5 mL, IM)
   - Fractional IPV-3 (0.1 mL, Intradermal)
   - Vitamin A 1st dose (1 lakh IU)
6. **At 16 to 24 Months**:
   - MR 2nd dose (0.5 mL, SC)
   - DTP 1st Booster (0.5 mL, IM)
   - OPV Booster (2 drops, Oral)
7. **At 5 to 6 Years**:
   - DTP 2nd Booster (0.5 mL, IM)
8. **At 10 Years & 16 Years**:
   - Td (Tetanus & adult Diphtheria) (0.5 mL, IM)
